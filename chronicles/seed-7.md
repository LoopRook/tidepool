# The pool of seed 7

*60 days, as recorded by the resident naturalist.*

- Day 1. Twenty-five Pinstriped Shadefins and counting.
- Day 1. The Pinstriped Shadefins (Umbripteryx lineata) have reached fifty. The algae may have opinions about this.
- Day 2. Counted twenty-five Olive Peersnouts today; they are doing well for themselves.
- Day 2. The first kill I have witnessed in the pool: the Dappled Barrelplume (Magnocauda stellata) took a Pinstriped Shadefin.
- Day 3. The Magenta Pipscales have crashed, from 23 to 8 in less than a day.
- Day 3. Over a hundred Pinstriped Shadefins today. The pool is starting to look like theirs.
- Day 5. The last Dappled Dawdlebelly (Lentosoma maculatum) is gone. It lasted four days and never numbered more than 10.
- Day 8. One Dappled Barrelplume has now made ten kills, the most of any creature I have followed.
- Day 8. The first death from old age: an Olive Peersnout (Scopichthys galbinus), aged eight days.
- Day 8. Hard times for the Olive Peersnouts (Scopichthys galbinus): barely five remain of the eighteen I counted recently.
- Day 9. Old age took an Olive Peersnout today, at a remarkable eight days.
- Day 9. I have stopped looking for the Olive Peersnout (Scopichthys galbinus); it is extinct. A run of nine days, peaking at 25.
- Day 10. A Dappled Barrelplume ate one of its own. Hunger makes poor manners.
- Day 10. Old age took a Freckled Tidesnout today, at a remarkable nine days.
- Day 11. The oldest creature I have yet recorded, a Dappled Barrelplume of ten days, died quietly today.
- Day 12. I have stopped looking for the Magenta Pipscale (Minutipinna purpurea); it is extinct. A run of eleven days, peaking at 23.
- Day 14. A venerable Pinstriped Shadefin has died at thirteen days. Nothing in the pool has lived longer.
- Day 14. The oldest creature I have yet recorded, a Pinstriped Shadefin of fourteen days, died quietly today.
- Day 19. The pool's first new species: the Red-striped Murkgill (Umbripinna rubrovirgata), split from the Pinstriped Shadefin (Umbripteryx lineata). It is a different colour altogether.
- Day 20. Old age took a Pinstriped Shadefin today, at a remarkable fourteen days.
- Day 24. Somewhere in the pool, a 10th-generation hatchling. The founders would hardly know it.
- Day 25. Struck the Dappled Barrelplume (Magnocauda stellata) from the list of the living. Twenty-five days, and at best fifteen of them.
- Day 28. Twenty-five Red-striped Murkgills and counting.
- Day 33. No Freckled Tidesnouts left in the pool. Thirty-two days from first sighting to last; at their height there were 23.
- Day 37. The Red-striped Murkgills (Umbripinna rubrovirgata) now number fifty, a new high.
- Day 40. The oldest creature I have yet recorded, a Pinstriped Shadefin of fifteen days, died quietly today.
- Day 44. The Red-striped Murkgills (Umbripinna rubrovirgata) now number a hundred. At this point they are less a species than a weather system.
- Day 57. Hard times for the Pinstriped Shadefins (Umbripteryx lineata): barely six remain of the nineteen I counted recently.

## At the close

174 creatures alive, 3 of 8 recorded species surviving, deepest lineage 19 generations. The commonest creature is the Red-striped Murkgill (*Umbripinna rubrovirgata*), 171 strong.
